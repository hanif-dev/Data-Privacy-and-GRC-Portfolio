# Data Privacy and GRC Portfolio

Welcome to my professional portfolio showcasing projects in **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)**, Data Privacy, and Privacy Engineering. 

This repository contains practical analyses, policy drafting, and regulatory mapping designed to ensure organizational compliance with global data protection frameworks while mitigating cyber risks.

## 📋 Table of Contents & Projects

Here are the key projects included in this repository. Click on the PDF files in the repository above to read the full reports.

### 1. Regulatory Legal Analysis & Mapping
* **[HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO Comparative Analysis]**
  * *Focus:* Cross-mapped medical data protection standards. Compared US sectoral laws (HIPAA/HITECH Act penalty tiers) with EU fundamental human rights frameworks (GDPR) and ISO 25237 definitions for pseudonymization and data integrity.
* **[GDPR Purpose Limitation & Data Minimisation]**
  * *Focus:* Deep-dive legal text analysis on GDPR Article 5, Article 25, Article 89, and relevant Recitals, establishing the legal boundaries of Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default.

### 2. Privacy Engineering & Architecture
* **[Zer-0 Automotive Privacy Statement (CCPA & GDPR)]**
  * *Focus:* Drafted a public-facing Privacy Statement for an automotive tech company ensuring compliance with US Laws (FTC Section 5, CCPA). Conducted a secondary analysis to highlight and alter specific clauses required for EU GDPR compliance (Articles 25.1 and 32.1).
* **[Network Identifiers & DOA Privacy Implications]**
  * *Focus:* Analyzed the historical development of IEEE 802.11 MAC addresses (from static hardware to MAC Randomization). Evaluated the severe privacy threats and GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure) conflicts posed by persistent identifiers in the Digital Object Architecture (DOA).

### 3. Corporate Privacy & Ethics
* **[Corporate Terms of Service vs. UN Human Rights]**
  * *Focus:* Conducted a comparative privacy assessment between Meta (Facebook) and DuckDuckGo. Evaluated their data harvesting practices directly against the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR), specifically mapping terms to the Preamble and Articles 3, 4, 12, and 19.
